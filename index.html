Yibo Wang

Welcome to my homepage!

I am currently an Associate Professor at the School of Automation Science and Engineering, Xi'an Jiaotong University. My research focuses on data-driven control, cyber-physical systems, and embodied intelligent robots. Previously, I held postdoctoral positions at Carleton University (with Prof. Shichao Liu), POSTECH (with Prof. Poogyeon Park), and The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (with Prof. Nan Guan).

Email: yibowang@xjtu.edu.cn

Open Positions

I am always looking for highly motivated Ph.D. students, Master's students, and outstanding undergraduates to join my research group. Candidates should have a strong interest in data-driven control, backed by a solid foundation in mathematics and programming. If you are interested in working with me, please feel free to drop me an email with your CV and transcripts.
